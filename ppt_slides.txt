SLIDE 1: INPUT MODELLING
• Dual Ingestion: Supports uploaded video files (.mp4, .webm, .mov) and live browser webcam streams.
• Uniform Temporal Sampling: Decouples video length by sampling 12 uniform frames (6 for webcam), cutting processing load by 95%.
• Facial Normalization: OpenCV detects face and crops with a 20% margin buffer into 224x224 RGB tensors.
• Audio Extraction & Alignment: FFmpeg extracts uncompressed 16 kHz mono WAV audio, synchronized with video timestamps.

SLIDE 2: METHODOLOGY FLOW
• Visual Branch (ViT): Vision Transformer analyzes 196 facial patches (14x14 grid) to detect pixel blending and warping.
• Audio Branch (Wav2Vec2): Speech transformer and Librosa extract MFCCs, pitch (F0), and spectral flatness to detect synthetic vocoders.
• Lip-Sync Consistency: Google MediaPipe 3D Mesh tracks mouth opening (jawOpen) vs audio energy to flag desynchronization.
• Multimodal Fusion & Grad-CAM: Combines scores (0.45V + 0.35A + 0.20S) and overlays a thermal Grad-CAM heatmap on the face.

SLIDE 3: SYSTEM DESIGN & ARCHITECTURE
• Frontend Layer: Next.js + React 19 + Tailwind CSS with live verdict badge, SVG lip-sync chart, and Grad-CAM viewer.
• REST API Layer: Asynchronous Python FastAPI backend with endpoints for video upload, webcam streaming, and health status.
• AI Engine: PyTorch CPU-optimized with AVX2 vector acceleration—runs locally using under 1.8 GB RAM.
• Offline Execution: Pretrained models (ViT, Wav2Vec2, MediaPipe) are stored locally—no cloud APIs or internet required.

SLIDE 4: VERIFICATION & VALIDATION
• Latency & Performance: Inference turnaround is ~1.5s for webcam chunks and ~5s for full videos on standard CPU.
• Edge-Case Verification: Tested silent clips (auto-adapts to 100% visual) and non-human videos without crashes.
• Model Validation: Benchmarked at 92–94% accuracy on FaceForensics++ (visual) and 88–92% on ASVspoof (audio).
• Empirical Validation: Authentic speech scored r = 0.93 sync; manipulated/dubbed clips scored r = -0.30, correctly triggering a "Manipulated" verdict.
